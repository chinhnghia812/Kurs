# Troubleshooting

## Rate read fails

Confirm Mainnet RPC availability and the contract ID in the README.

## Publishing is rejected

Use the configured administrator wallet and rebuild the transaction after any
timeout or sequence-number change.

## USDC payment fails

Verify network, issuer, sender balance, merchant trustline, and quote expiry.

## Freighter does not open

Use Chrome, unlock the extension, permit the deployment domain, and reconnect.
